# Destruction Racing

A modern spiritual successor to *Destruction Derby* (1995) and *Destruction Derby 2* (1996) for Windows PC: the same arcade gameplay (derbies in the Bowl, Wreckin' Racing on a figure-8 with a blind crossover, spin and wreck scoring, the DD damage zones), with modern visuals. Keyboard only. Built with **Godot 4.7 .NET (C#)** and Jolt physics.

The cars are generated by this project: nine fictional late-70s and 80s cars, three each from America (Brougham, Homestead, Interceptor), Europe (Nordvik, Adler, Volant) and Japan (Hikari, Ryujin, Kitsune), modelled from signed-distance shapes in `tools/carsmith`. Nineteen named drivers race them, each in a signature livery. Their damage is layered: zone crush, a dent volume, crumple folds, paint wear, and parts that come off as physics debris. The engine sounds are synthesized (`tools/enginesynth`).

## Running it
1. Install the **.NET 8 SDK** and put **Godot 4.7.2 .NET** in `.engine/godot/` (see [`docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md)).
2. Double-click **Play Destruction Racing.cmd** in the project folder: it builds and starts the game. The main menu has the Championship (Wreckin' or Stock Car, four divisions, three save slots), Single Event (Destruction Derby, Wreckin' Racing, Stock Car Racing, Duel, Total Destruction), Time Trial, Practice, Options and Credits. The first launch asks for your key layout.
   Other launchers, for development:
   - **Play Driving Test.cmd**: free driving with the tuning panel.
   - **Damage Lab.cmd**: real crashes into a parked car, to look at the damage.
   - **Pileup Test.cmd**, **Bowl Look.cmd**, **Car Studio.cmd**, **Fog Test.cmd**, **Engine Test.cmd**: look-dev and stress scenes.

The keys are shown on screen. Driving: arrow keys, plus the keys in the on-screen guide.
