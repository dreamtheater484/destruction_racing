# Destruction Racing

A modern spiritual successor to *Destruction Derby* (1995) and *Destruction Derby 2* (1996) for Windows PC: the same arcade gameplay (derbies in the Bowl, Wreckin' Racing on a figure-8 with a blind crossover, spin and wreck scoring, the DD damage zones), with modern visuals. Keyboard only. Built with **Godot 4.7 .NET (C#)** and Jolt physics.

The cars are generated by this project: fictional late-70s and 80s European, Japanese and American cars (the Kitsune, Brougham and Nordvik so far), modelled from signed-distance shapes in `tools/carsmith`. Their damage is layered: zone crush, a dent volume, crumple folds, paint wear, and parts that come off as physics debris. The engine sounds are synthesized (`tools/enginesynth`).

## Running it
1. Install the **.NET 8 SDK** and put **Godot 4.7.2 .NET** in `.engine/godot/` (see [`docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md)).
2. Double-click a launcher in the project folder; each one builds and starts a scene:
   - **Play Derby.cmd**: Destruction Derby in the Bowl: pick the light (afternoon, golden hour, night), your car and the division, then 20 cars, last car running wins. After the event, V shows the replay.
   - **Play Wreckin.cmd**: Wreckin' Racing on the figure-8.
   - **Play Driving Test.cmd**: free driving with the tuning panel.
   - **Damage Lab.cmd**: real crashes into a parked car, to look at the damage.
   - **Pileup Test.cmd**, **Bowl Look.cmd**, **Car Studio.cmd**, **Fog Test.cmd**, **Engine Test.cmd**: look-dev and stress scenes.

The keys are shown on screen. Driving: arrow keys, plus the keys in the on-screen guide.
