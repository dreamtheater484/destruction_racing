# Development guide

## Toolchain
- **Godot 4.7.2 .NET** is installed locally in `.engine/godot/` (not versioned). Its export templates are in `%APPDATA%\Godot\export_templates\4.7.2.stable.mono`.
- **.NET SDK 8**.
- **NuGet restore is offline only** (`NuGet.Config`): it uses Godot's bundled packages plus the local package cache.

## Automated gameplay checks
- **Handling scenarios:** `headless.ps1 -Scene res://scenes/test/DriveTest.tscn -UserArgs '--scenario=accel'` (also `brake`, `circle`, `slalom`, `turnaround`, `keys`).
- **DD Code tactics T1–T5** (plan §4.7, each must succeed in ≥ 60% of trials): `headless.ps1 -Scene res://scenes/test/DdCodeTest.tscn -UserArgs @('--tactic=all','--trials=10') -Filter DDCode`. Add `--verbose` for per-trial notes, `--hits` for spin-assist details, `--tactic=probe` for raw impact measurements.
- **Derby sims** (plan §17.4): `headless.ps1 -Scene res://scenes/test/DerbySim.tscn -UserArgs @('--sims=30') -Filter 'Sim\]'`. The PlayerProxy bot drives the player's car through the keyboard path; the report goes to `out/sims/`. `--profile=skilled`, `--seed=N`, `--dump=60` (print every car's state every 60 s), `--division=1..4`, `--strategy=hide|ram|reverse|farm` (a dominant-strategy test bot in the player's car; each derby then prints the bot's final damage). Experiments: `--nosqueeze`, `--nocover`, `--greywalls` (the M1–M3 wall), `--reartough=X`, `--opening=SCALE,SECONDS` (the opening damage ramp), `--attackshare=X`. Each early player wreck (≤ 60 s) prints the car's state and where its front damage came from. Run batches in parallel processes with different `--seed` ranges and merge them with `derby_report.py`.
- **Race sims** (plan §17.4): `headless.ps1 -Scene res://scenes/test/RaceSim.tscn -UserArgs @('--sims=10') -Filter 'Sim\]'`. `--kind=wreckin|stockcar`, `--layout=figure8|oval`, `--reverse`, `--profile=average|racer|hunter|skilled|compare` (`compare` runs the racer and the hunter bot on every seed), `--skill=X`, `--laps=L`, `--cars=N` (`--cars=1` = the bot alone: a clean-lap check), `--dump=S`, `--wallhits`, `--playerhits` (every hit on the player with its source), `--pitlog` (every pit decision, stop and release), `--pittest` (the bot stops on its first pass). Each race (and each derby) also prints its damage by source and the DD Code used in the field (brake-checks and the tailgaters they caught, squeezes, tailgating, dodges, blocks).
- **Race experiments** (RaceSim): `--nodefence`, `--nolag`, `--notailgate`, `--nobrakecheck`, `--nosqueeze` switch a DD Code behaviour off; `--nobarrels` drops the cushions' barrel colliders; `--scheadway=S`, `--scdraft=X`, `--scpace=X`, `--scbumplap=N` (Stock Car following gap, extra drafting, AI pace, the lap the bumping starts); `--leaderhunt=BEHIND,AHEAD` (metres); `--proxypitline=P`; `--attackscale=X` (the AI's division aggression, whatever `--division` says); `--wallhits` marks hits on a cushion; `--outdir=out/sims/x` (where the report goes); `--endurance` (the 100-lap check).
- **Time Trial targets:** `headless.ps1 -Scene res://scenes/test/TimeTrialSim.tscn -UserArgs @('--layout=oval','--sims=5')` drives the skilled and the average bot round every car and checks the medals (gold reachable by "skilled", not by "average"); `--reverse`, `--car=<id>`, `--laps=L`, `--writetargets` (merges the targets of the cars it ran into `assets/speedway/time_trial/<layout>_<dir>.json`, so `--car=<id>` updates one car).
- **Reproducible:** the same command gives the same results (the impact system sorts Jolt's contacts). A seed run alone plays out differently from the same seed inside a batch, because Jolt's history differs; replay a batch with `--sims=N` up to that seed.
- **Model cars vs the grey box:** every scene uses the generated cars (the nine of `CarCatalog`; their size, wheel positions and collider come from the glb). `--greybox` turns every car back into the M1 grey box, for comparing tuning against the M1 baselines; the DD Code test takes `--model=<car>` for both cars.
- **Per-car handling** (M5): each model's mass, power, drag, grip, steering and toughness come from its five ratings (`CarInfo.Tuning`, `CarInfo.DamageToughness`). `--sametuning` gives every car the shared baseline and toughness 1 (A/B runs). `--car=<id>` picks the player's (or the proxy's) model.
- **Car balance** (plan §9.3): `--randomcars` (derby and race, the sims too) puts every AI driver and the proxy in a random model, so each car meets every archetype. Every headless event prints one `[CarResult]` line per car (`--carresults` adds them to a windowed run); `balance.sh` and `balance_report.py` turn them into the report. `--ratingscale=mass:X,tough:Y,...` (keys mass, accel, top, grip, steer, tough, and fwdfront and fwdrear for the front-drive grip) overrides the balance knobs of `RatingScale` for tuning runs; `--carstats=volant:7,7,8,3,5` (several separated by `;`) sets a car's speed, acceleration, handling, weight and toughness. In races the `[CarResult]` line also gives `spun` (times the car was spun after a hit), `stuck` (put back on the track) and `longeststop`.
- **Driver taunts:** `--taunts=off|some|all` sets how often drivers talk on the radio (default some); `--radiotest` shows one banner during the countdown, for captures (use `--fixed-fps 60` so the capture frame lands inside its four seconds).
- **Pileup stress test** (plan §16, at every milestone): `headless.ps1 -Scene res://scenes/test/PileupTest.tscn -Filter Pileup` checks Jolt stability (no launches, nothing through the floor, no jitter once settled, no cars inside each other) and the cost per physics tick. With rendering, effects and debris: `--visual --perf=2 --quit` (windowed). `--seed=N` jitters the start (M8: seeds 1–12 pass). After the release every car is held still, and a car that misses the pile is held still at once, so the checks see the crash, not a car driving off. It prints the cause of a failure (`fast:`, `spin:`, `moving at the end:`, `inside:`) and every tick gap over 30 ms (`slow tick:`, with the .NET GC counts).
- **Watch the bot play:** run `res://scenes/derby/Derby.tscn` or `res://scenes/race/Race.tscn` with `--proxy` (and `--seed=N`). The race also takes `--kind=wreckin|stockcar`, `--layout=figure8|oval`, `--reverse`, `--laps=L`, `--overview[=SIZE]` (top-down view of the layout) and `--view=Chase|ChaseFar|Bumper|InCar`; `res://scenes/race/TimeTrial.tscn` takes `--layout`, `--reverse`, `--car` and `--proxy`. `--speedwaycheck` prints the layout's lap length and pit lane.

## Command‑line hooks (after `--`)
- `--scene=res://...`: the scene to open instead of the default.
- `--capture=<path>` with `--capture-frame=N`: save a screenshot, then quit.
- `--quit-after=N`: smoke test that quits after N frames.
- `--perf[=S]`: print frame time (mean and 1% low), draw calls, triangles and VRAM every S seconds (default 5); since M8 also the GPU time of the frame, the physics frame (mean of Godot's per-second worst and the worst), the cars' drivers per physics tick (the AI budget), the dents and volume uploads per frame (the deformation budget) and the particles emitting now.
- `--ab=F1,F2,...` (with `--perf`): every other window turns one feature off, the next in the list each time (`Tools/PerfAb.cs`: dof, smoke, shadows, soft, lights, particles, ssao, ssil, ssr, vfog, glow, blur, film, debris, debrisshadow), so neighbouring windows show its cost. **For A/B between builds or shaders, run with `--fixed-fps 30`**: the event then plays out the same every run (the windows without the feature match to 0.01 ms GPU), so the windows can be compared run against run; the fps column is then meaningless, the GPU column is real. In real time no two runs see the same cameras, and a run-to-run difference of 30% means nothing.
- `--scale3d=X` (performance runs): the 3D resolution as a factor of the window; `--resolution 2560x1440 -- --scale3d=1.5` renders 4K. With `--usersettings --devuser` the run uses `user://dev/settings.json` (set the preset there, e.g. Ultra).
- `--shot-every=S --shot-dir=out/x`: save a screenshot every S seconds (stress scenes).
- `--greybox`: every car is the grey box (see above).
- `--maxcontacts=N`: contacts each car reports to the impact system (default 48; for experiments).
- `--pinned=S` (derby): how long a car may rock in place before it counts as immobilised (default 20 s; for experiments).
- `--preset=afternoon|golden|night` (derby, race, Time Trial): the venue's light; `--division=1..4`; `--car=<id>` (one of the nine).
- `--autoskip` (derby, windowed): fast-simulate to the results; `--autoreplay`: open the replay at the results (replay checks), `--autoreplay=highlights` the highlights reel instead.
- `--replaycheck` (with `--autoreplay`, derby or race): the replay self-test. It rebuilds every car's damage from its journal and compares it bit for bit with the car as the event left it (`[Replay] self-test: 20/20 cars match`).
- `--audiolog` (derby): print what the audio loaded and every announcer line as it is said.
- `--marks-debug` (derby): show the raw ground-marks texture on the floor; `--marks-dump=out/x.png`: save it when the derby closes.
- `--autobake=res://...` (editor only): bake the lightmaps, save the scene, quit. It waits until that scene is the one open (the editor may first restore the last session's scene).
- `--loadtime` (race): prints the time to the first frame and to the green light, after launch or after an R restart; `--loadtime=restart` presses R itself once and quits after the reloaded race starts. The front end and the derby print their first frame too (after launch, or after the menu's START, R or leaving an event).
- `--fliptest` (race): puts the player's car on its roof 4 s after GO (the rollover and the righting).
- **The front end** (M7): the game starts at `res://scenes/menu/FrontEnd.tscn` (Boot's default). The player's settings (`settings.json`), championships (`career/slot1..3.json`) and progress (`profile.json`: the best division reached, the champion flag, the Total Destruction best) live in `user://`; Time Trial records stay in `user://timetrial/`.
  - Settings only apply when the game runs without `--scene` (or with `--usersettings`), so dev, test and sim runs keep the defaults.
  - `--devuser`: settings, championships and the profile go to `user://dev/` instead (test runs). `--windowed`: keep the window whatever the settings say.
  - `--keys=Enter,Down,w2,Escape`: presses keys one by one from 1.5 s on, across scene changes (`--menukeys` is the same).
  - `--fakecareer=ROUNDS[,stockcar][,win]` (with `--devuser`): opens the hub of slot 3 with that many rounds played on random results (`win`: the player tops every round), e.g. to see the season review.
  - `--autocontinue`: on a results screen, runs the first action after 4 s (CONTINUE in a championship).
  - The event scenes read the front end's `Session.Request` when they are the main scene; started directly they take the hooks above.
- **Championship sims:** `RaceSim` takes `--division=1..4` (the AI's pace and aggression of that division, and the player's roof); every `[CarResult]` line also gives `who` (the driver's index in `Drivers.All`, −1 = the player) and `wrecks` (wrecks caused).
- `--pittest` (race with `--proxy`): the bot stops on its first pass.
- **M8 hooks:**
  - `--duel[=NAME]`, `--totaldestruction` (derby started directly): the Duel (against that driver, or the first) and Total Destruction.
  - `--nofilm`: the film look off in a dev run (before/after captures of the colour grade, grain and vignette); `--motionblur=PERCENT`: the motion blur of a dev run (default 40).
  - `--mixlog` (events): the mix meter. Every bus is captured after its fader; every 30 s and at the end it prints each bus's integrated level (400 ms blocks, gated as in BS.1770, plain RMS: about LUFS), its loudest 3 s and its peak. Run it in real time: with `--fixed-fps` the game clock outruns the audio, and so does the announcer's queue (lines expire before they can play).
  - `--audiolog` (derby) now also prints when the event finishes (`[Commentary] finished at …`).
  - `--lodreport[=CAR]`: every car's triangles at full detail and at each LOD Godot generated, then quits; with a car id, also that car's meshes, triangles and materials.
  - `--allocprobe`: lists what the .NET runtime allocates (large-object heap and small objects, by type; from the runtime's allocation ticks) and the GC counts and pauses, every 20 s and at the end of the pileup test. It found the M8 GC hitches.
  - Movie Maker (Godot's own, no ffmpeg): `Godot --path . --write-movie out/clip/f.png --fixed-fps 30 -- --scene=… --quit-after=N` writes numbered PNGs (and a WAV); `out/m8/derby_clip.webp` was made from them with PIL.
  - PhotoStudio `--wear=X`: the livery's age, e.g. to check the rust from age.
- **Garbage in hot code** (M8): Godot's C# tracks every wrapper it makes (a Dictionary, a StringName or NodePath made from a string, a Variant key) until it is disposed, and every tick's leftovers made 50–130 ms full GCs. In code that runs every tick or frame: no `IntersectRay` (use a `RayCast3D` and `ForceRaycastUpdate`), no string literals for `SetShaderParameter`, `Input.IsActionPressed` (use `Controls.Name`), `GetNode` and metadata (use a `static readonly StringName` / `NodePath`), no `foreach` over an `IReadOnlyList<T>` (it boxes the enumerator), and no new arrays per frame (the engine voices push fixed 128-frame blocks).
